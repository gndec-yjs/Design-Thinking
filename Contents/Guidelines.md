# Design Thinking – Guidelines

This page provides **key links and instructions** for students to follow the Design Thinking project workflow.

## 1. Project Workflow & Stages

Follow the Design Thinking process throughout the course:

* **Stage 1 – Observe:** Field observation and identification of a real civil engineering problem
* **Stage 2 – Define:** Stakeholder analysis, empathy tools, root-cause analysis and problem statement
* **Stage 3 – Ideate:** Generation, screening and selection of solution concepts
* **Stage 4 – Prototype:** Development of physical or digital prototype
* **Stage 5 – Test & Improve:** Feedback, validation, iteration and final design

#### **To View Detailed Workflow:** [**Click Here**](Project_Workflow.md)

## 2. Deliverables & Submission

Students are required to maintain documentation throughout the Design Thinking process.

* All deliverables should be **typed and properly documented**.
* Photographs, sketches, diagrams and field evidence should be clearly labelled and captioned.
* Each team should maintain a record of observations, decisions, feedback and iterations.
* Final submissions should be in **PDF format** unless otherwise specified.
* Follow the prescribed file naming convention for all submissions.

## 4. Final Project

The final project should present the complete journey from **problem identification to the final validated design solution**.

It should include:

* Field observations and problem evidence
* Stakeholder and empathy analysis
* Root-cause analysis
* Problem statement and HMW statement
* Ideation and concept selection
* Prototype development
* Testing and feedback
* Iterations and improvements
* Final design and justification

## 5. Notes

* Select a **real and observable civil engineering problem**.
* Do not jump directly to a solution; first understand the users and the root cause.
* Maintain evidence of every stage through photographs, sketches, diagrams and records.
* Clearly document how feedback resulted in improvements to the prototype.
* Team members should contribute meaningfully to all stages of the project.
* Use appropriate engineering principles, standards and references wherever applicable.
