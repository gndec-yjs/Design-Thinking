## 📌 The Task

Each student shall prepare **one individual poster** presenting the complete Design Thinking journey for the **civil engineering problem they have already identified and documented**.

This poster will form part of your **final design problem solution for the Design Thinking course**. It must demonstrate how you progressed from understanding the problem to developing and evaluating a suitable solution.

## 🧭 Your Design Thinking Journey

Organise your poster around these four broad stages:

### 1. EMPATHISE — Understand the Problem

Present your problem title, field observations, photographs, stakeholders, user needs, pain points and relevant evidence.

### 2. ANALYSE — Define the Real Problem

Show your root-cause analysis, key insights, final problem statement, constraints and **How Might We (HMW)** question.

### 3. SOLVE — Generate and Develop Ideas

Present multiple solution ideas, justify your selected concept and include a suitable sketch, drawing, model or digital prototype.

### 4. TEST & IMPROVE — Validate the Solution

Show how the proposed solution was or could be tested, relevant feedback, key learning and improvements. Clearly distinguish actual testing from proposed testing.

## 🎨 Poster Guidelines

- Prepare **one poster per student**, preferably in **A3 size**, portrait or landscape.
- Highlight the **problem title, problem statement, Design Thinking process and final proposed solution**.
- Use concise text, photographs, diagrams, sketches and visuals to communicate your journey effectively.
- You may use **Canva, ChatGPT, Gemini, PowerPoint, AutoCAD, SketchUp** or any other suitable design or AI tool.
- Your observations, analysis and decisions must be your own. Verify AI-generated content and acknowledge external sources and AI-generated visuals where applicable.
- Include your **name, roll number and course details**.
