## 🧭 Report Structure and Required Content

### 1. DELIVERABLE 1 — Problem Identification and Field Observation (Unit 1)

Document one real civil engineering problem identified through field observation.

Include:
- Problem title, background, location and context.
- Field observations and problem logbook.
- Original photographs, sketches, basic measurements and notes, wherever applicable.
- Initial understanding of the problem.

### 2. DELIVERABLE 2 — Empathy, Problem Definition and Root-Cause Analysis (Unit 2)

Demonstrate how the problem was understood from the perspective of relevant stakeholders and users.

Include:
- Stakeholder identification and mapping.
- Interviews or survey inputs, wherever feasible.
- Empathy map/persona, user needs, pain points and expectations, as appropriate.
- 5-Why and/or Fishbone analysis.
- Final evidence-based, solution-neutral problem statement.
- Relevant constraints and a **How Might We (HMW)** question.

### 3. DELIVERABLE 3 — Ideation, Concept Selection and Prototyping (Unit 3)

Show how alternative solutions were generated, evaluated and developed.

Include:
- Multiple solution ideas and ideation techniques used.
- Concept screening and feasibility considerations.
- Weighted decision matrix with criteria, weights and scores.
- Selected concept and justification.
- Suitable sketch, drawing, layout, physical/digital prototype, mock-up or model.

### 4. DELIVERABLE 4 — Testing, Validation and Final Design Solution (Unit 4)

Demonstrate how the proposed solution was or could be tested and improved.

Include:
- Testing methodology or proposed testing plan.
- User, peer or expert feedback, wherever obtained.
- Feedback record and analysis.
- Iterations and improvements.
- Final design concept and justification, considering cost, safety, sustainability and ease of implementation, as relevant.

**Important:** Clearly distinguish between activities actually conducted and those proposed for future testing. Do not present hypothetical interviews, feedback or test results as actual findings.

## 📌 General Instructions

- **Individual submission is compulsory.** Every student must prepare and submit a separate report.
- The report must be based on the student's own identified problem and individual poster. Maintain consistency between the two.
- Document the complete process rather than merely describing the final solution.
- Use original field photographs, observations, sketches, measurements and supporting evidence wherever applicable.
- Explain the reasoning behind the problem definition, root causes, selected concept and proposed improvements.
- Do not fabricate observations, measurements, stakeholder responses, survey results, testing or feedback.
- AI and digital tools may be used for assistance. Verify generated content and acknowledge AI-generated material and external sources, wherever applicable.
- Include references for external information, images, data, standards and other sources used.
- Include a copy of your **individual Design Thinking poster** in the appendices.
