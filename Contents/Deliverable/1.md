## Course Code: ESCE102

## **Deliverable Requirements**

| **Sr. No.** | **Component** | **Description** |
| ---------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| 1 | **Problem Background** | Briefly describe the selected site and the observed situation. |
| 2 | **Field Observation** | Record what was actually observed at the site. |
| 3 | **Photographic Evidence** | Include relevant photographs with captions. |
| 4 | **Sketches & Measurements** | Provide sketches, annotated photographs and basic measurements where relevant. |
| 5 | **Users / Stakeholders** | Identify the people affected by the observed problem. |
| 6 | **Observation → Symptom → Problem** | Distinguish between what is observed, the resulting symptom, and the possible underlying problem. |
| 7 | **Initial Problem Statement** | Write a clear, specific and **solution-neutral** problem statement. |
| 8 | **Questions for Further Investigation** | List questions that need to be explored during the next stage. |

## **Examples of Suitable Problems**

Students may consider problems related to:

- Dampness, seepage or deterioration in buildings
- Corrosion and deterioration of reinforced concrete
- Poor thermal comfort, lighting or ventilation
- Waterlogging and drainage problems
- Pedestrian safety and traffic circulation
- Construction dust, noise and worker safety
- Construction and demolition waste
- Excessive material wastage
- Sustainable and recycled construction materials
- Affordable and lightweight construction
- Energy-efficient and climate-resilient buildings
- Accessibility for elderly or differently-abled users
- Road, bridge and transportation infrastructure
- Flood, landslide and other disaster-resilience challenges

> **These are only examples. Students are encouraged to identify problems through their own field observation.**
